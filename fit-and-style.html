Fit & Style

Fit and style should work together.

A sharp suit is not only about size. The cut, balance and details must work with the person wearing it.

Fit Direction

The right fit should look sharp without feeling uncomfortable.

TCT keeps fit guidance simple: Slim Fit, Modern Fit and Classic Fit. The final result can then be refined based on your body shape and preference.

Slim Fit

Best for customers who want a sharper, closer silhouette. Slim Fit gives stronger shape through the waist, sleeves and pants, but should still allow the customer to sit, walk and move naturally.

Modern Fit

TCT’s safest recommended starting point for most customers. Modern Fit keeps a clean shape while giving more comfort and mobility than Slim Fit. It works well for business, weddings and regular formal use.

Classic Fit

Best for customers who prefer traditional comfort, a more relaxed shape, or extra ease around the chest, waist, arms and legs. Classic Fit is especially useful for mature customers or larger builds.

Style Components

Elements that shape the final look.

A suit’s presence comes from the combined effect of fit, fabric, jacket shape, pants line, shirt pairing and finishing details.

01

Shoulder line

The shoulder creates the first impression. It should look balanced and clean without appearing oversized or restrictive.

02

Jacket length

Jacket length affects proportion. A well-balanced jacket can make the wearer look taller, sharper and more put together.

03

Lapel width

Lapel width should suit the customer’s build and the suit’s purpose. A balanced lapel is usually safest for long-term wear.

04

Pants taper

Pants should flow cleanly from the waist through the thigh and down to the shoe. Overly tight pants can reduce comfort and look less premium.

05

Shirt collar

The collar frames the face and affects how the suit looks with or without a tie. It should sit cleanly under the jacket.

06

Overall balance

The best result is when jacket, pants and shirt work together instead of each item looking separate.

TCT Recommendation

When unsure, start with Modern Fit.

Modern Fit works for most customers because it looks clean, professional and comfortable. During fitting, TCT can make the direction sharper or more relaxed.
