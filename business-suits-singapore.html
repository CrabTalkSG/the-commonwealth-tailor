What to know

Useful details for business suit customers.

Best colours for business

Navy, charcoal and medium grey are the safest starting colours because they are versatile, professional and easy to match with shirts and ties.

Comfort for long days

For customers who sit, commute or move around often, Modern Fit is usually the safest balance between sharp shape and comfort.

Build a work wardrobe

Pair one suit with several shirts and tailored pants to create more combinations without needing too many full suits at the beginning.
