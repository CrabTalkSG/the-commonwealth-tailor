FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to book an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so we can prepare fitting time and fabric options for you.

Can I choose everything online?

You can enquire and view packages online. Final measurements and fabric selection are done in person for accuracy.

How much deposit is required?

Production usually starts after order confirmation and deposit. The balance is payable upon final collection.

Is alteration included?

Necessary fitting adjustments will be reviewed during fitting. Customer-requested changes after confirmation may be chargeable.
