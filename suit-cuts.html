Suit Cuts & Style Guide

Choose the right suit shape, then perfect the fit.

A good suit is not only about size. The cut affects how sharp, formal, comfortable or relaxed the final look feels. TCT guides customers through the main fit options and suit styles before measurements are taken in person.

Core Fit Options

The main suit cuts customers should understand.

These fit options are the easiest way for customers to describe the look they want. The final result is still refined during measurement, fitting and adjustment.

Sharpest silhouette

Skinny Fit

Very close, fashion-forward and highly tapered.

Best for customers who want a very sharp, contemporary look. The jacket, sleeves and pants are cut closest to the body, so it is less forgiving and should be chosen carefully.

Modern close fit

Slim Fit

Sharp, fitted and contemporary without looking oversized.

Best for leaner builds and customers who like a clean body line. It is closer through the chest, waist, sleeves and pants, but should not pull or feel restrictive.

TCT recommended default

Modern Fit

Clean, balanced and comfortable.

The safest starting point for most customers. It gives shape at the waist and a neat pants line while still allowing movement for work, weddings and daily wear.

Timeless formal comfort

Classic Fit

Traditional, roomier and elegant.

Best for customers who prefer more room through the chest, waist, arms and pants. It is comfortable, conservative and suitable for mature or formal business dressing.

Comfort-first

Relaxed Fit

More ease, softer structure and extra movement.

For customers who prioritise comfort over sharp tapering. It is useful for bigger builds, long events, long workdays or customers who dislike tight tailoring.

Tailoring Style Directions

Style references for a stronger personality.

These are useful when customers already know the kind of presence they want. TCT can use them as style references during consultation.

Soft European elegance

Italian-Inspired Cut

Softer, stylish and more fashion-forward.

Usually associated with softer shoulders, a slimmer body line and a lighter visual feel. Suitable for customers who want elegant style without looking too corporate.

Structured executive look

British-Inspired Cut

Structured, confident and formal.

A stronger, more formal direction with defined shoulders and a controlled waist shape. Suitable for executives, ceremonies and customers who want presence.

Easy business comfort

American-Inspired Cut

Straighter, simpler and comfortable.

A more relaxed business direction with less aggressive waist shaping. Best for customers who want a simple, conservative and easy-wearing suit.
