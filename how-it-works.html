Customer Process

How custom tailoring works at TCT

Start online, visit for measurements and material selection, then return for fitting and final collection.

01

Enquire Online

Message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what the suit is for, when you need it and your preferred style.

02

Receive Guidance

We share price guide, Standard vs Premium explanation and suitable package suggestions.

03

Book Appointment

Choose a fitting slot. Appointments are recommended so we can prepare the right fabric options.

04

Measurement & Material Selection

Visit TCT for measurements, fabric selection, style consultation and order confirmation.

05

Deposit & Order Confirmation

After details are confirmed, production begins with deposit payment and documented order details.

06

Fitting & Adjustments

Try on your garment at TCT. Any necessary fitting adjustments are recorded and arranged.

07

Final Collection

Collect your finished suit after final approval and balance payment.

08

Aftercare & Future Orders

We keep your measurements for smoother future orders such as shirts, pants or another suit.

Before Your Appointment

What customers should prepare.

  • Know the occasion and required date
  • Bring reference photos if you have a desired look
  • Wear comfortable clothing for measurement
  • Bring dress shoes if pants length is important
  • Allow around 45 minutes for measurement and fabric selection
