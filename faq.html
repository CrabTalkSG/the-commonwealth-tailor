FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful answers before booking your TCT fitting appointment.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so we can prepare fitting time and suitable material options for you.

Can everything be done online?

Enquiry, guidance and appointment booking can be done online. Measurements and material selection are done in person for accuracy.

How much is a 2-piece suit?

Standard 2-piece suits start from S$398. Premium 2-piece suits start from S$788.

How much is a 3-piece suit?

Standard 3-piece suits start from S$518. Premium 3-piece suits start from S$988.

What is the difference between Standard and Premium?

Standard is ideal for everyday tailored elegance. Premium uses finer materials and offers a more elevated presentation for important occasions.

What should I bring for fitting?

Bring reference photos if you have a desired look. For pants, dress shoes are helpful for checking length.

Is deposit required?

Production begins after order confirmation and deposit. Balance is payable upon final collection.

Are alterations included?

Necessary fitting adjustments will be reviewed during fitting. Customer-requested changes after confirmation may be chargeable.

Do you offer a satisfaction guarantee?

Yes. TCT stands by the quality of our craftsmanship and fitting experience. If you are not completely satisfied, we will review the fit and arrange necessary corrections. If we cannot make it right, we will offer a money-back guarantee.
