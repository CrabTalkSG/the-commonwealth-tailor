Collection Comparison

Standard for everyday confidence. Premium for elevated presence.

Both collections are designed for a proper made-to-measure experience at TCT. Choose based on occasion, fabric preference and the level of finish you want.

Standard Collection

Everyday tailored elegance.

Smart, refined and practical tailoring for office wear, interviews, meetings, events and first tailored suits.

  • Sharp starting price
  • Clean made-to-measure fit
  • Ideal for regular business use
  • Good choice for groomsmen, teams and group orders
Premium Collection

Elevated luxury tailoring.

Higher-grade fabrics, finer finishing and stronger visual presence for weddings, executives and special occasions.

  • Richer fabric feel
  • Finer finishing and stronger presence
  • Stronger drape and presence
  • Recommended for grooms and key events

How TCT helps you choose

Tell us your occasion, timeline and preferred look. We will guide you on colour, fit, lapel style, fabric feel and whether Standard or Premium is the better match before you visit for measurements.

Choosing Guide

Which collection should I choose?

For work

Start with Standard Collection. Navy, charcoal or dark grey works best for daily business use.

For weddings

Choose Premium if the suit is for the groom or main event. Standard is suitable for groomsmen packages.

For executives

Premium is recommended for better fabric feel, presentation and a stronger first impression.
