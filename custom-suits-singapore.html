What to know

Useful details for custom suit customers.

What makes a good custom suit?

A good custom suit should balance shoulder line, chest comfort, waist shape, sleeve length, pants break and the overall silhouette. TCT guides customers through these decisions clearly so the final look feels sharp without being uncomfortable.

Who this is best for

Business owners, working professionals, grooms, groomsmen, graduates, interview candidates and customers who want one reliable suit that can cover multiple important occasions.

TCT recommendation

For most customers, we recommend starting with Modern Fit because it gives a clean tailored shape while still allowing comfortable movement in Singapore weather.
