Tailoring Knowledge

A professional guide for first-time and repeat customers.

This guide explains what TCT looks at before recommending a suit: occasion, fit, fabric, proportion, comfort, timeline and final styling.

1. Occasion comes first

A wedding suit, business suit, interview suit and daily work suit should not be treated the same way. TCT starts by understanding where the suit will be worn, how often it will be used, whether photos are important, and whether the customer needs a safe classic look or a stronger personal statement.

2. Fit is about proportion

A suit should follow the body without fighting it. Shoulder balance, jacket length, waist suppression, sleeve length, pants taper and trouser break all affect the final impression. TCT guides customers towards Slim, Modern or Classic fit based on their frame and comfort preference.

3. Fabric affects presence

Fabric is not only about colour. Weight, texture, drape and structure determine whether a suit feels practical, formal, luxurious or relaxed. For Singapore, breathability and comfort matter, but important occasions may still require richer fabric presence.

4. Details create character

Lapel shape, lining, button selection, pocket style, shirt collar, pants pleats and cuffs all change the finished look. Customers do not need to know every term — the appointment is designed to guide those decisions clearly.

5. Timeline should be realistic

Customers should enquire early, especially for weddings and major events. A comfortable timeline allows proper consultation, production, fitting review and any required adjustment before the final date.

6. The final suit should match the person

The best suit is not always the most fashionable one. It should match the customer’s build, role, lifestyle and confidence level. TCT focuses on creating a result that looks sharp and feels wearable.

Before You Visit

What customers should prepare before a fitting.

Know your occasion

Tell us whether the suit is for work, wedding, interview, photoshoot, dinner, solemnisation or regular office wear.

Bring reference photos

Reference photos help us understand your preferred style, even if you do not know the exact tailoring terminology.

Think about comfort

Some customers prefer sharp and fitted; others prefer more room. TCT will advise how to balance style and movement.

Share your required date

Timeline is important. Let us know your deadline early so we can advise the most suitable order plan.
