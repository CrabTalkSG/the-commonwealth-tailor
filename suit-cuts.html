Suit Cuts & Fit Guide

Choose the right cut before perfecting the fit.

TCT keeps the selection simple for customers. Start with a fit direction, then refine the exact shoulder, waist, sleeve, pants and comfort details during your fitting appointment.

Ask About FitView Collections

Core Fit Options

Four simple cuts most customers need.

For customer-facing selection, TCT should focus on Slim Fit, Modern Fit, Classic Fit and Relaxed Fit. These are easiest to understand and cover most body shapes, occasions and comfort preferences.

Sharp modern silhouette

Slim Fit

Sharp, close-fitting and modern.

Best for leaner builds, younger customers and customers who prefer a contemporary silhouette. The jacket and pants sit closer to the body for a sharper look.

Recommended for most customers

Modern Fit

Clean, balanced and comfortable.

Our recommended everyday cut. It gives shape without feeling too tight, making it suitable for business suits, weddings and first tailored suits.

Traditional formal comfort

Classic Fit

Timeless, formal and slightly roomier.

A more traditional cut with additional comfort through the chest, waist, arms and pants. Ideal for mature customers and conservative formal wear.

More room and movement

Relaxed Fit

Comfort-first with extra ease of movement.

Designed for customers who prioritise comfort, movement and long hours of wear. The silhouette is less tapered and more forgiving.

Style Directions

Additional style directions for customers who want a stronger look.

Italian, British and American-inspired directions are useful during consultation when customers describe the type of presence they want.

Stylish European feel

Italian-Inspired Cut

Softer, stylish and more fashion-forward.

A refined silhouette with a softer shoulder feel, slimmer body line and elegant visual flow. Best for customers who want a stylish European direction.

Structured executive presence

British-Inspired Cut

Structured, confident and executive.

A more formal style direction with stronger shoulders, cleaner waist shape and a powerful business presence. Suitable for executives and important occasions.

Simple comfortable business look

American-Inspired Cut

Straighter, simpler and comfortable.

A comfortable business silhouette with a natural shoulder and less aggressive shaping. Best for customers who prefer a conservative and easy-wearing suit.

TCT Recommendation

Modern Fit should be the default starting point.

It is the safest commercial choice because it works well for business, weddings, first suits and most Singapore customers.
