Measurements

Measurements taken properly in person.

You do not need to measure yourself. TCT records body measurements, posture notes and style preferences at the fitting appointment.

Jacket

Shoulder, chest, waist, hip, sleeve length, jacket length, bicep, wrist, armhole, back width and posture notes.

Pants

Waist, seat, thigh, knee, calf, opening, inseam, outseam, front rise and back rise.

Shirt & Vest

Neck, shoulder, chest, waist, hip, sleeve, length, bicep, cuff, vest chest, vest waist and vest length.

Fitting Notes

Numbers are important, but posture matters too.

During measurement, TCT also records fit preference, shoulder slope, posture, pants wearing position and other details that affect how the garment sits on the body.
