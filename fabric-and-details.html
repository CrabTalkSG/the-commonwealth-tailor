Fabric & Details

The small details are what make a suit feel personal.

A good suit is not only about measurements. Fabric, drape, lapel, buttons, lining, pants style and shirt pairing all affect how the final look feels.

Before You Choose

Fabric direction should match your occasion, climate and how often you wear the suit.

TCT helps customers choose practical, flattering and occasion-appropriate materials instead of making decisions based only on colour.

Fabric weight

Singapore weather makes fabric weight important. Lighter and breathable options are better for daily business use, while richer fabrics can feel more elevated for weddings and formal events.

Colour choice

Navy and charcoal are the safest for business and first suits. Grey, blue, brown and statement colours can be considered for weddings, dinners and personal styling.

Pattern direction

Plain fabrics are versatile. Subtle checks, pinstripes or textured weaves can add character when customers want something more distinctive.

Suit Details

Details we guide you through during your fitting.

You do not need to know all these terms before visiting. This page helps customers understand what will be discussed at the appointment.

01

Lapel style

Notch lapels are versatile and business-friendly. Peak lapels create stronger presence and are often suitable for weddings, formal events and premium looks.

02

Buttons

Two-button jackets are the most common and balanced. Button colour and finish can change the overall feel from conservative to more distinctive.

03

Lining

Lining can be kept subtle for business suits or used as a personal accent for wedding and occasion suits.

04

Pockets

Flap pockets are classic. Jetted pockets feel cleaner and more formal. Patch pockets are more relaxed and less common for formal suits.

05

Pants style

TCT can guide on waist fit, taper, length, cuffs, pleats and overall proportion depending on shoes, body shape and preferred silhouette.

06

Shirt pairing

White and light blue shirts are the safest foundation. Custom shirts help complete the suit and improve the overall formal presentation.

Detail Guide

Common tailoring details customers can consider.

These are the areas where TCT can help customers refine the garment from a basic suit into a more complete personal wardrobe piece.

Single-breasted jacket

The safest and most versatile jacket style for most customers. It works for office wear, interviews, weddings, dinners and everyday formal occasions.

Double-breasted jacket

More formal and statement-driven. Best for customers who already understand tailoring or want a stronger, more confident silhouette.

Two-piece suit

Jacket and pants. This is the most practical suit format and easiest to wear repeatedly for work, interviews and general events.

Three-piece suit

Jacket, pants and vest. This gives a more complete formal look and is especially suitable for weddings, ceremonies and premium occasions.

Flat-front pants

A clean and modern pants style suitable for most business and formal looks. It keeps the front line simple and sharp.

Pleated pants

Useful for customers who want more comfort, a more classic menswear look, or additional room around the hip and thigh.

Singapore Climate

Fabric choices should make sense for local weather.

Singapore’s heat and humidity make comfort important. For daily business wear, customers usually benefit from breathable fabrics, practical colours and clean styling that can be worn repeatedly. For wedding and formal occasions, a richer fabric feel may be worth considering because photographs, stage presence and overall impression matter more.

  • Choose lighter, practical fabric for regular office use
  • Choose richer texture and drape for weddings or formal events
  • Use navy, charcoal or grey for maximum versatility
  • Consider custom shirts to complete the suit cleanly
