The 3 core cuts

Choose the fit that matches your style and comfort.

These descriptions are simplified so customers can understand the difference quickly before coming down for a fitting appointment.

Sharpest silhouette

Slim Fit

Sharp, sleek and more tapered through the body.

Slim fit gives the sharpest and most presentable look of the three. Expect a stronger V-shaped taper and a closer fit along the arms and legs. It should still feel appropriate and comfortable, but movement will be more limited than the other cuts.

Best for: presentations, interviews, meetings, proms and customers who want a sharper silhouette.

TCT recommended default

Modern Fit

Balanced shape with better mobility and everyday comfort.

Modern fit keeps a flattering V-shaped taper, but does not hug the body as much as slim fit. It is roomier, easier to move in and ideal for customers who want a smart look without sacrificing comfort.

Best for: most customers, regular business wear, weddings, warm Singapore weather and those who are often on the move.

Timeless formal comfort

Classic Fit

Traditional, comfortable and slightly roomier.

Classic fit puts comfort first. It offers more room in the arms, legs, chest and waist while still following the body shape so it does not look boxy. It is especially suitable for larger-framed or more mature customers who prefer a timeless look.

Best for: mature customers, larger builds, conservative business dressing and anyone who prioritises comfort.

TCT Recommendation

Modern Fit is the safest starting point for most customers.

It offers the best balance between clean tailoring, comfort and versatility. During the fitting, TCT can always taper more or relax specific areas according to your preference.
