Collection Comparison

Standard for refined daily wear. Premium for elevated occasion presence.

Both collections follow the same guided TCT fitting experience. The difference is the fabric feel, finishing level, visual presence and how important the occasion is.

Standard Collection

Refined everyday confidence.

A smart and practical made-to-measure option for customers who want a polished fit, clear value and versatile formalwear for regular use.

  • Clean made-to-measure silhouette
  • Best for work, interviews and regular business wear
  • Strong entry point for first tailored suits
  • Suitable for groomsmen, teams and group orders
Premium Collection

Elevated occasion presence.

A more premium expression of tailoring for customers who want richer fabric feel, stronger drape and a more distinguished appearance.

  • Richer fabric handle and visual depth
  • More elevated finishing and presentation
  • Recommended for grooms and key events
  • Ideal for executives, business owners and milestone occasions

How TCT helps you choose

Tell us your occasion, timeline and preferred look. We will guide you on colour, fit, lapel style, fabric feel and whether Standard or Premium is the better match before you visit for measurements.
