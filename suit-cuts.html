The 3 core cuts

Choose the fit that matches your style and comfort.

These descriptions are simplified so customers can understand the difference quickly before coming down for a fitting appointment.

Slim Fit

The sharpest and most presentable look of the three. Expect a stronger V-shaped taper and a closer fit along the arms and legs.

Best for: presentations, interviews, meetings and customers who want a sleek, contemporary look.

Modern Fit

A balanced fit that keeps a smart V-shaped silhouette without hugging the body too much. It offers better range of motion and overall comfort.

Best for: most customers, regular workwear, weddings and customers who are often on the move.

Classic Fit

Comfort-first and slightly roomier through the chest, waist, arms and legs while still following your body shape so it does not look boxy.

Best for: mature customers, larger builds and customers who prefer a timeless, more comfortable fit.

TCT Recommendation

Modern Fit is the safest starting point for most customers.

It offers the best balance between clean tailoring, comfort and versatility. During the fitting, TCT can always taper more or relax specific areas according to your preference.
